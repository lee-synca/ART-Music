Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026

This is the official website of A.R.T. We keep things simple: we don't sell your data, and we only collect what helps us understand how the site is used.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to see how people find and use the site — for example, which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located (country/region), the type of device, and which link brought you here. This data is aggregated and anonymous — it doesn't identify you personally.

We only receive personal details (like your name or email) if you choose to give them to us — for example by emailing us or signing up for updates.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets small cookies in your browser to measure visits. You can:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or
  • Install Google's Analytics Opt-out add-on to opt out of Analytics everywhere.

Links to other services

Our site links to platforms like Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and ticketing sites. Once you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.
